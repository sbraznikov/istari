# Ticket quality

What the refiner checks in every ticket of this repository (istari spec
section 4.5). Edit it to fit the project.

- The acceptance criteria are verifiable: each one can be checked by running something or reading a result.
- There are no contradictions or ambiguities.
- The size matches the scope and does not exceed the allowed one.
- The modules and APIs it mentions exist in the code.
- It does not duplicate or contradict another ticket.
- "Out of scope" says what will not be done, so that the work stops at the right place.
